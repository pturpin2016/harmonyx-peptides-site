PRIVACY

How we handle your information.

HarmonyX LLC collects only what we need to review research order requests, send invoices, and follow up with you. This page is a practical notice, not legal advice.

What we collect

When you submit an order request or a contact message, we may collect:

  • Name, email, and phone number
  • Shipping address, if you choose USPS
  • Products requested, quantities, promo code, and notes
  • Fulfillment choice (Northern CA pickup or shipping)
  • A Cloudflare Turnstile token used only to confirm you are human

If you check “save my info on this device,” that contact and address data stays in your browser’s local storage. We cannot read that copy from our servers.

How we use it

We use this information to review requests, confirm availability, send order emails and draft invoices, arrange pickup or shipping, and answer questions. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for medical advice or marketing lists.

Where it is stored

Order details are stored in Google Sheets / Google Drive associated with our business account and emailed to Paul and Thabby at research@harmony-x.com (and the internal notification address). Email and invoice copies may remain in Gmail and Drive until we delete them.

How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed to fulfill requests, maintain business records, and answer follow-up questions. You may ask us to update or delete contact details that are no longer needed.

Age

This catalog is restricted to research purchasers 21 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
