REFUNDS & RETURNS

Refunds and returns.

Our products are research reagents, so we can't take back vials once they've been opened or unsealed. If something goes wrong with your order, we'll make it right. Here's how.

Damaged, incorrect, or missing items

If an item arrives damaged, or we sent the wrong item or quantity, email research@harmony-x.com within 7 days of delivery or pickup. Include your invoice number and a photo. We'll replace the item or refund it.

Lost packages

If USPS tracking shows no delivery within 10 business days of shipping, contact us. We'll send a replacement or issue a refund.

Cancellations

You can cancel any request before payment. After payment, you can cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships or is picked up.

Unopened returns

Unopened items with the original seal intact may be returned within 14 days of delivery or pickup for a refund of the item price. Return shipping is the purchaser's responsibility. Email us first for return instructions. We can't accept opened or unsealed vials, or items that have been stored out of our control in a way we can't verify.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Shipping charges are refunded only when the problem was our error, or the package was lost.
