FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short answers so you know exactly how HarmonyX works before you send a request.

Are these for human use?

No. HarmonyX products are for laboratory and research use only — not for human or veterinary consumption, and not as drugs, supplements, or cosmetics. Access is limited to qualified research purchasers 21 or older.

How does ordering work?

Add vials to your request and submit. No payment is taken on the site. Paul and Thabby review every request, usually within 24 hours, then confirm availability and send a draft invoice.

How do I pay?

Zelle, Apple Cash, or Venmo after we confirm. We do not take credit cards. Payment details appear on the invoice.

Where is local pickup?

Free pickup by appointment in Northern California: Lathrop, Manteca, Stockton, and Tracy. USPS Priority is $15, or free on orders of $400 or more (merchandise total after any promo).

How long does it take?

We typically reply within 24 hours. In-stock vials can be picked up or shipped after payment. Special-order lots may take longer — we will tell you before you pay.

How do I get a COA?

Every batch is COA-tested. Open the current lot PDF on the Certificates of Analysis page, or email research@harmony-x.com if you need a lot that is not listed yet.
