Bacteriostatic Water 3 mL

Sterile bacteriostatic water for laboratory reconstitution of lyophilized research peptides. Sized for single-vial work.

Overview

Bacteriostatic water is sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a preservative. In a research laboratory it is used as a reconstitution diluent. It is a supply item, not a research peptide.

In plain language

This is the water used to dissolve a freeze-dried vial so the contents can be measured on the bench. The benzyl alcohol helps keep an opened multi-use vial from growing microbes during a short laboratory window. It is not a treatment, supplement, or drug for people or animals.

What researchers use it for

  • Aseptic reconstitution of lyophilized research peptides
  • Aliquoting and volume-matched bench work
  • Short-term refrigerated storage of reconstituted solutions per laboratory SOP

Handling

Use aseptic technique. Wipe the stopper before each puncture. Do not use if the solution is cloudy, discolored, or the seal is broken.

Storage

Store sealed vials protected from light per the label. After first puncture, refrigerate and follow laboratory SOP.

Research use only

Offered for laboratory and research use only. Not for human or veterinary consumption.
